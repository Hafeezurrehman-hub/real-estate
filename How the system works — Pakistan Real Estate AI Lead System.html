The problem

An enquiry arrives at 11:23 PM. The reply goes out at 10 AM.

By morning the buyer has messaged three other agencies, and one replied first. It is not one problem but seven:

1. Nobody answersNights, Sundays, Jummah
2. Local languageBuyers don't write in English or in numbers
3. Time wastedAgents chase people who only check rates
4. Invented pricesAn AI that makes up a price is a liability
5. DuplicatesOne buyer becomes three records
6. Forgotten follow-upsNobody chases on the right day
7. No visibilityThe owner can't see who is dropping the ball
The pipeline

Five stages, no human involved, under 30 seconds

1 · CAPTUREWebsite form, portal email, Facebook Lead Ads, WhatsApp
2 · NORMALISEPhone to one format, budget to PKR, timeline to days, duplicate check
3 · ENRICHAgent by area and capacity; match against the agency's real inventory
4 · AI QUALIFYScore, tier (HOT / WARM / COLD), shortlist, next action
5 · ACTWhatsApp reply, team alert on HOT, database update, calendar booking

Alongside it: a reply handler (book a visit, ask rates, opt out, speak to a human), follow-up engine, visit reminders, daily report and a global error handler.

The hard part: language

One real sentence, fully understood

"Bahria Enclave mein 1.5 crore tak ghar chahiye, do teen mahine mein lena hai"
AreaBahria Enclave
Property typeHouse
Budget1.5 crore = PKR 15,000,000
Timeline"do teen mahine" = 90 days
Just researchingNo
Lead score90 / 100 HOT

Three parsers written from scratch: timeline (Roman Urdu number words), budget (crore / lakh), and phone numbers (every Pakistani format).

Built to be trusted

What stops it from going wrong

Never invents a priceThe AI only sees projects from the agency's own inventory. No match? It says so.
Sorts serious from browsingBudget, area, timeline, payment mode; browsing language lowers the score.
One buyer, one recordPhone normalised first, then an upsert, so history is kept.
Follow-ups, not spamEach lead has its own decaying schedule. Opt-out is permanent.
Local rules in codeNo visits on Friday prayer time or Sundays. Asia/Karachi time.
Fails loudlyStrict structured output. Any error is logged and alerted.
The visit, booked automatically

The whole exchange, with no one awake

Buyer · 11:23 PM: Bahria Enclave mein 1.5 crore tak ghar chahiye…
System · 11:23 PM: 3 matching options from the agency's inventory, with real prices. "Visit ke liye VISIT likhein."
Buyer · 11:25 PM: visit

The owner sees it on a phone dashboard: HOT leads not yet booked, and follow-ups due today. A written summary arrives by WhatsApp and email every evening at 8.
